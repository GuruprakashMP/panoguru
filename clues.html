The 20-second method (do this every round)
1
Which side of the road is the car driving? Look at the centre line and oncoming cars. Left = only 24 of the 107 countries in this game (see Driving side). This alone removes most of the world.
2
Find any text. Shop boards, road signs, number plates, posters. First identify the script (Latin, Cyrillic, Arabic, Thai …), then the language from special letters and common words.
3
Road furniture. Line colours (yellow centre = Americas, parts of Asia; white = most of Europe), sign shapes (yellow diamond warnings = Americas, Australia, Japan, SE Asia, Ireland; red triangles = Europe, Africa, India), bollards, guardrails, poles.
4
Landscape and light. Dry/red soil, eucalyptus, birch, palms, rice paddies, snow. Sun low in the north at midday = southern hemisphere; low in the south = northern hemisphere (see Landscape & sun).
5
Cars and people. Plate colour/shape, car brands (Ladas in Russia, Protons in Malaysia, Holdens in Australia, tuk-tuks in Thailand/Sri Lanka/India, Kei cars in Japan), school bus colours, taxi colours.
6
Zoom into the Google car itself (look down and around): snorkel, roof rack, tape, escort car, low picture quality. These are country-specific (Google car section).
7
Decide quickly, then refine. Country first (worth most of the points), then region: coast vs mountains, big city vs village, road number prefixes on signs, phone area codes, town names on boards.

Scoring reminder: 5,000 × e−km/1493. Guessing the right country but 500 km off still gives ~3,600; the wrong continent gives ~0. Country first, always.

Driving side

Drives on the LEFT (the 24 covered countries in this game) solid: United Kingdom, Ireland, Malta, Japan, Australia, New Zealand, India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Hong Kong, Macao, South Africa, Lesotho, Eswatini, Botswana, Kenya, Uganda, Bermuda.

Everything else drives on the RIGHT: all of continental Europe (incl. Faroe, Greenland, Iceland), the Americas (incl. Puerto Rico, Curaçao), Russia and Central Asia, the Middle East, Turkey, Taiwan, South Korea, Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos, Mongolia, Philippines, Ghana, Nigeria, Senegal, Rwanda, Tunisia, Egypt, Madagascar, Guam, N. Marianas, American Samoa.

Tip: if no car is visible, use road markings (centre line vs. edge) and which side the arrows/bollards with red reflectors face; or the position of the Google car over the lane.

Landscape, climate and the sun
  • Sun position solid: in the northern hemisphere the midday sun is in the south (shadows point north); in the southern hemisphere it is in the north. Near the equator it is overhead. Use the compass in the game: find the sun, read the direction, decide hemisphere. Long shadows + low sun = high latitude or early/late day.
  • Eucalyptus (grey-green, peeling bark): Australia; also planted in Spain/Portugal (Galicia!), Brazil, South Africa, Kenya, India, Ethiopia, California.
  • Birch + pine + lakes: Finland, Sweden, Russia, Baltics, Canada. Spruce/fir + mountains: Norway, Alps, Canada. Rolling green + hedges + stone walls: UK, Ireland, Normandy.
  • Palm trees + humid: SE Asia, Brazil coast, Caribbean, Florida, Kerala/Sri Lanka. Coconut + rice paddies: Indonesia, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Sri Lanka, Kerala, Bangladesh.
  • Red soil: Brazil interior, Kenya/Uganda, Australia outback, Ghana/Nigeria, southern India. Olive trees + dry hills + terraces: Spain, Italy, Greece, Portugal, Tunisia, Israel, Jordan. Vineyards: France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Chile, Argentina (Mendoza), South Africa, Australia.
  • Cactus + desert: Mexico, SW USA, Peru coast, Chile north (Atacama, no plants at all), Israel/Jordan, UAE/Oman/Qatar (sand, modern roads, SUVs).
  • Steppe, treeless, yurts: Mongolia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan (with big mountains). Tundra/fjords: Norway, Iceland (no trees, black lava, moss), Faroe.
  • Snow + concrete poles + Ladas: Russia; snow + wooden red houses: Sweden/Norway/Finland.
  • Houses: red tile roofs = Mediterranean/Balkans/Latin America; black slate roofs = Galicia/N. Portugal/N. Spain; wooden painted houses = Nordics/Russia/Canada; concrete flat roofs with rebar sticking out = Mexico, Middle East, India, Africa; corrugated iron = Africa, SE Asia, Australia (red/green tin roofs), NZ.
  • Skies: the camera generation changes sky colour more than the real sky does; do not over-read it.
