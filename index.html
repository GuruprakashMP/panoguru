How to play
  • Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap the arrows to move. ⟳ View redraws the picture if it goes black, ↺ Start returns to the start.
  • Press Open map, tap where you think you are, then Guess.
  • Score = 5000 · e−distance / 1493 km. Within 25 m = perfect.
